# Background flower images

Drop photographs here and they appear automatically as soft background
imagery on the home, testimonial and guide pages. No code change needed —
the site reads whatever is in this folder at build time, so adding a file
and redeploying is enough.

## What to add

- **Format:** PNG or WebP with a transparent background works best, because
  the images are blended into the page. A JPEG with a white background will
  show as a pale rectangle.
- **Size:** roughly 800–1600px on the long edge. They are rendered small and
  faint, so anything larger is wasted bandwidth.
- **Naming:** anything, as long as the extension is .png, .webp, .jpg or
  .jpeg. Files are used in alphabetical order.

## Where to get them

**Best option — photograph your own.** You have jaswand and marigold at the
studio during the season. Your own photographs are original, cost nothing,
carry no licence risk, and look like your studio rather than like stock.

**Free for commercial use:** Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay all allow
commercial use without attribution.

**Do not use watermarked stock previews.** Images from pngtree, Freepik
previews, Shutterstock previews and similar carry visible watermarks and are
not licensed for use. They would appear watermarked on the live site.
